    Keyboard Fantasia

    Keyboard Fantasia is a musical space shaped with love and dedication by Pradeep. This platform brings together some of his finest keyboard performances, fulfilling a dream he carried for years: to revive old melodies and present new ones, all played and recorded by himself.

    A Graduate in Electronics & Communication from CET Class of 85 with a passion for music from younger days. After retiring as a Senior Scientist from Vikram Sarabhai Space Centre after 36 years, having worked in the areas of Test & Evaluation, Design of Modules and their Test Systems for Launch Vehicles, Pradeep chose to return to the passion that stayed with him throughout his life… Music.

    His 50 year musical journey began with the Bulbul in School, an instrument many from today’s generation may barely remember, later graduating to the Keyboards. What started as a simple curiosity grew into a vast personal collection of songs, carefully played, recorded, and preserved over the years. Now, he is ready to share that treasure with everyone.

    Keyboard Fantasia is not just a website—it is a window into pure, heartfelt music. A chance to rediscover forgotten sounds, relive nostalgia, and enjoy melodies created with sincerity.

    Welcome... a beautiful musical chapter unfolds.

    How to Play Music

    1

    Cassettes: Click the cassette slots (C-1 to C-6) on the center console to load audio track collections.

    2

    Deck Controls: Use the physical deck controls (PLAY, PAUSE, FWD) to manage playback of the loaded tracks.

    3

    Manage Songs: Use the side panel song manager to enable, disable, or delete individual tracks from a collection.

    4

    Photo Viewer: View album art or custom photos in the small display above the speaker while music plays.
